# Tiny Desk AI

### One portrait. Your moment on the desk stage.

[Tiny Desk AI](https://tinydeskai.net/) is an independent AI video creator that turns one portrait into a personalized desk-concert performance inspired by the Outstanding Tiny Desk format.

Put yourself—or an adult who has agreed to appear—in the lead singer's place, then save a short performance with sound for a birthday surprise, a group chat, or a social post.

**[Create your desk-concert video →](https://tinydeskai.net/)**

## Meet Solo Cast

Solo Cast starts with one portrait and replaces the lead singer in the configured performance reference. The backing performers and sax player remain from the reference, keeping the focus on your chosen headliner.

You choose the person. The reference provides the performance structure, camera movement, and timing. There is no editing timeline or prompt to write.

## How it works

1. **Choose your lead singer.** Upload a clear, front-facing portrait with visible eyes and even lighting.
2. **Create the performance.** Sign in, choose a one-time video pack, and start generation.
3. **Save your video.** Play the completed result, download the MP4, and share it in your preferred app.

Video packs are one-time purchases with no subscription. Visit the website for current pricing and availability.

## Before you create

Use photos of yourself or adults who have given permission. Clear portraits work best; avoid sunglasses, masks, heavy filters, and dark or blurry images.

AI-generated appearance, clothing, and movement can vary. Exact resemblance and frame-by-frame matching are not guaranteed, so review the result before sharing.

Tiny Desk AI is an independent product and is not affiliated with NPR or any featured artist. References to Tiny Desk and Outstanding describe the creative format; they do not imply endorsement or access to official concert recordings.
